About

About Moses Mastery

Moses Mastery is a Shopify growth studio led by Moses Daniel. We design, optimize and grow online stores: better design, faster pages, clearer offers, search visibility and paid ads that send the right people to a store that is ready for them.

Who is behind Moses Mastery?

I’m Moses Daniel, the founder. I started Moses Mastery to help online brands get more from the store they already have, through strategic branding, conversion-focused design and high-performance development.

I started solo and now work with a team of specialists, because strategy, conversion optimization, user experience and brand have to work together as one system. I stay hands-on in every project, leading strategy and getting into design and marketing, because that is where a store’s results are decided.

What do we do?

  • Shopify store design and development
  • Conversion rate optimization and store audits
  • Shopify SEO and Google Merchant Center
  • Meta and Google ads
  • Etsy SEO and shop optimization
  • AI automation for eCommerce stores

Who do we work with?

Online brands across many niches, including pet, apparel, drinks, restaurants, skincare and supplements. You can see examples of the stores we have worked on on the projects page. We work with clients worldwide, and parts of this site are available in German.

How do we work?

  • We start with a free scan. The store scanner shows what can be measured from outside your store, and a personal review adds design, offer and checkout.
  • Fixed quotes. You get a clear scope and price before work starts. Store packages are one-time project fees, and marketing packages are monthly.
  • Honest expectations. We don’t guarantee sales or rankings, because no one can. We show you what we change and why.

Where to find us

You can find Moses Mastery on LinkedIn, X, Instagram and Product Hunt. You can also find our Google Business Profile. Worked with us? We would appreciate a review on Google.
