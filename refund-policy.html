Refund Policy.

In short

I want you to be happy with the work. If something is not right, tell me first and I will fix it. This policy explains what happens if we cannot resolve it, and what is and is not refundable.

Free items

The strategy call, the store review and the revenue calculator are free, so there is nothing to refund.

Fixed-price projects

  • Before work starts: if you cancel after paying a deposit but before I begin, the deposit is refunded in full, less any payment-processor fees.
  • During the project: if you cancel part-way, you pay for the work completed up to that point and the remainder of any prepayment is refunded.
  • After delivery: I will correct anything that does not match the agreed scope, free of charge, for 14 days after delivery. Once the project is approved and delivered, fees are non-refundable.

Monthly and retainer services

Monthly services (such as ad management or ongoing optimization) can be cancelled with 14 days’ written notice before the next billing date. The current month is not refunded once the work for it has started, and there are no refunds for past months.

What is not refundable

  • Ad spend paid to Meta, Google or any other platform, which is charged by them and not by me.
  • Third-party costs such as themes, apps, domains, licences and stock assets.
  • Work you asked me to stop using, or changes outside the agreed scope.
  • Results that differ from your expectations when the agreed work was delivered as described (see section 5 of the Terms & Conditions).

How to ask for a refund

Email info@mosesecom.com with your name, the project and what went wrong. I reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
