Privacy

How this site handles data.

The short version

This site counts visits so I can see what works. It does not use advertising cookies, it does not sell data, and it never records what you type into a form.

What is recorded when you visit

  • The pages you open, how long you stay, and how far you scroll.
  • Which buttons and links you click (for example Book a call, WhatsApp or email), and whether you start or send the contact form. The text you type is never recorded.
  • Your device type, browser, operating system, screen width, time zone, and the site you came from.
  • Your approximate location (country, region and city). To work this out, your browser asks a geolocation service (ipwho.is, or geojs.io or ipapi.co if the first is unavailable), which sees your IP address as any website you visit would. I never receive or store your IP address.
  • Technical signals that help me fix the site: page speed, and errors your browser hits.

This data is stored in a database I control (Supabase). It carries a random identifier kept in your browser storage, not your name or email. The identifier is not a cookie and is not shared with advertisers.

Session recordings and heatmaps

If switched on, this site uses Microsoft Clarity to show me anonymised recordings and heatmaps of how pages are used. Everything typed into form fields is masked. Clarity is not started for visitors in European time zones. Microsoft’s privacy statement is at privacy.microsoft.com.

When you contact me

If you send the contact form or the calculator review form, your answers, name and email are delivered to me by Web3Forms and are also saved in my database (Supabase) so a request is never lost. They are used only to reply to you. If you book a call, Calendly handles that booking under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • If your browser sends Do Not Track, no analytics are recorded. Automated visitors such as search crawlers and speed-test tools are not counted.
  • You can also block the tracker with any content blocker, and the site still works.
  • To ask what is held about you, or to have it deleted, email info@mosesecom.com and include the approximate date and device of your visit.

Who is responsible
