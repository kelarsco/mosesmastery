Shopify Speed Optimization: What Slows a Store on Mobile and How to Fix It

By Moses Daniel, founder of Moses Mastery

Quick answer

Most Shopify stores are slow on mobile for the same few reasons: oversized images, too many apps, heavy theme features and third-party scripts such as chat widgets and ad pixels. Fix those in that order, measure after each change, and keep only what earns its weight. Speed matters because shoppers leave slow pages before they see your products.

1. How do you measure Shopify store speed?

Test your store on mobile with Google PageSpeed Insights, because that is the view that matters most. Paste your homepage, a collection page and a product page, since each loads differently. Look at the largest content paint (how long the main content takes to appear), layout shifts and the list of opportunities. Google publishes “good” targets for these measures; as a rough guide, the main content should show in about 2.5 seconds or less.

Scores move a little between runs, so test several times and focus on the biggest items in the report instead of the headline number. You can also run our free store scanner to see your speed result next to your SEO, mobile and conversion checks.

2. Are images slowing your store down?

Oversized images are the most common cause, and the easiest to fix. A product photo uploaded at a very large size forces a phone to download far more data than it needs.

  • Resize images to the size they are actually shown at, before uploading.
  • Use modern formats such as WebP where your theme supports them.
  • Do not lazy-load the main image at the top of the page; load it first.
  • Lazy-load images further down the page.

3. Do too many apps slow a Shopify store?

Yes, often. Many apps add scripts that load on every page, even where they are not used. Review your app list and ask what each one does, whether you still use it, and whether the theme could do the same thing natively.

  • Remove apps you no longer use, and check that they left no code behind in your theme.
  • Replace several small apps with one tool, or with a built-in theme feature.
  • Test your speed before and after removing an app, so you know what it cost.

4. Can your theme be the problem?

Yes. Heavy sliders, autoplay videos, animations and many sections on the homepage all add weight. A simpler homepage usually loads faster and often converts better, because shoppers find the main message and products sooner.

  • Cut the hero slider down to one strong image or a single short video.
  • Remove sections nobody scrolls to.
  • Keep the theme updated, and avoid adding custom code that duplicates what it already does.

5. How do third-party scripts affect speed?

Chat widgets, ad pixels, review widgets, popups and analytics all load extra code, and together they can add up. You need some of them, but not all of them need to load immediately.

  • List every tracking and marketing script on your store.
  • Remove duplicates, such as the same pixel installed twice by two apps.
  • Delay non-essential scripts until after the page has loaded where possible.

6. What about fonts?

Too many fonts and font weights slow the first paint. Use one or two font families, only the weights you need, and let text show in a fallback font while the custom one loads.

Common speed mistakes

  • Chasing a perfect score instead of fixing the biggest items first.
  • Installing a “speed booster” app that adds its own scripts.
  • Testing only the homepage and ignoring product and collection pages.
  • Testing on a fast office connection instead of a typical phone.
  • Making many changes at once, so you cannot tell what helped.

Common questions

What is a good speed for a Shopify store on mobile?

Google’s guidance is for the main content to appear in about 2.5 seconds or less on mobile. Your real-world numbers depend on your theme, apps and visitors’ devices, so treat the lab score as a guide.

Why is my Shopify store slow even with a fast theme?

Usually because of apps, third-party scripts and large images added on top of the theme. The theme is only part of what loads.

Will deleting an app make my store faster?

Often, if the app loaded scripts on every page. Some apps leave code behind after removal, so check your theme files or ask a developer to clean up.

Does page speed affect Google rankings?

Google uses page experience as one of many signals, and speed also affects whether shoppers stay and buy. Do not expect speed alone to change your rankings.

How often should I test my store speed?

Test after every app install, theme change or big content update, and run a full check monthly.
