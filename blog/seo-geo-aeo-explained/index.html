SEO, GEO and AEO Explained for Shopify Store Owners

By Moses Daniel, founder of Moses Mastery

Quick answer

SEO, GEO and AEO are three ways of getting found online. SEO gets your pages ranked in search results like Google. GEO gets your brand mentioned in answers written by AI tools like ChatGPT. AEO gets your content chosen as the direct answer to a question. They overlap heavily, and for most Shopify stores the work is the same: clear pages, accurate details and real proof.

1. What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is making your pages easy for Google and Bing to find, understand and rank, so shoppers searching for what you sell click through to your store.

It works through keywords that match what people search, clear page titles and descriptions, a fast mobile site, useful content and links from other websites. Success looks like a higher position in the results and more clicks. It is the foundation for the other two. Our Shopify SEO guide covers the practical steps.

2. What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is making it more likely that AI tools, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI answers, mention or recommend your brand when they write an answer.

AI tools tend to rely on sources that are clear, factual and consistent. In practice that means plain-language pages, the same business details everywhere (your website, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile and review sites), real customer reviews and structured data. Success looks like your brand named in an AI answer, ideally with a link. No one controls what an AI tool says, so no one can guarantee a mention.

3. What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is writing your content so that it can be used as the direct answer to a specific question, in featured snippets, voice assistants and AI answer boxes.

It works through question-style headings, a short direct answer straight after each heading, and FAQ sections. Success looks like your sentence being the answer shown, instead of one blue link among many. This page is written that way on purpose: each heading asks the question and the first line answers it.

4. How do they differ?

SEOGEOAEO
Where you appearSearch results pagesAnswers written by AI toolsSnippets, voice and answer boxes
Main goalRank and get clicksGet mentioned or recommendedBe the quoted answer
Key tacticsKeywords, speed, links, contentConsistent facts, reviews, structured dataQuestion headings, short answers, FAQs

The newer two are less proven than SEO. The tools change often and there is no settled playbook, so be wary of anyone who sells guaranteed results.

5. Which should a Shopify store focus on first?

Start with SEO fundamentals, because GEO and AEO build on them. A store that is slow, unclear or missing basic information will struggle in all three. A sensible order:

  • Fix the basics. Page speed, mobile layout, clear titles and descriptions, and working policies. A free store scan shows where you stand.
  • Make product and collection pages clear. Plain descriptions, accurate prices and structured data. See our product page SEO checklist.
  • Answer real questions. Add short FAQs to product and policy pages using the questions customers actually ask.
  • Keep your details consistent. Use the same business name, website and description on your site and profiles.
  • Collect real reviews. Proof from real customers helps shoppers and gives AI tools something trustworthy to cite.

Common questions

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on the same foundations as SEO, and search results are still a major way people find stores. Treat GEO as an addition, not a replacement.

Can I guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my store?

No. AI tools decide what to mention and the sources they use change. You can make your store easier to understand and trust, but a mention can never be guaranteed.

Is AEO the same as GEO?

They are close cousins. AEO focuses on being the direct answer to a question, including in snippets and voice. GEO focuses on being mentioned in answers written by AI tools. The tactics overlap a lot.

How long does it take to see results?

It varies by store, competition and what is fixed, and no one can promise a timeline. Technical fixes can show up sooner than authority and reviews, which build over months.

Do I need all three?

You do not need to treat them as three projects. Strong SEO basics, clear answers and real proof cover most of what all three reward.
