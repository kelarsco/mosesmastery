How Much Does Shopify Store Design Cost? What Affects the Price

Quick answer

There is no single price for a Shopify store, because it depends on how much you want built and who builds it. The cost has two parts: what you pay Shopify and app providers every month, and what you pay for design and setup. This guide explains what drives each, so you can compare quotes properly. For current platform prices, check Shopify’s own pricing page, since plans change.

1. The costs that don’t depend on who designs it

  • Shopify plan. A monthly subscription, with different tiers and some transaction fees. See Shopify’s pricing page for current numbers.
  • Domain. A yearly fee, usually modest.
  • Theme. Shopify has free themes, and paid themes are generally a one-time purchase. Many stores are well served by a customized theme.
  • Apps. Reviews, email, subscriptions and others often carry monthly fees, which add up. Choose carefully.
  • Payment fees. A percentage per order from your payment provider.

2. What drives the design and setup price

  • Number of products and collections. Uploading, organizing and writing for a catalogue takes time.
  • Customization. Light theme styling is far less work than a custom layout or unique features.
  • Content. Who writes the copy, takes photos and prepares the brand? Those can be included or separate.
  • Features. Product bundles, subscriptions, custom forms, multi-language and multi-currency selling all add scope.
  • Integrations and migration. Moving from Etsy, WooCommerce or another platform means moving products, customers and redirects.
  • SEO and tracking. Proper analytics, structured data and redirects are easy to forget and costly to skip.

3. Template, freelancer, agency or DIY

DIY with a theme costs the least in money and the most in time. A freelancer or small studio usually offers a good balance of quality, communication and price. A larger agency suits complex projects and bigger budgets, but you pay for their overheads. If you’re deciding between the last two, read the guide on redesigning versus optimizing before you spend.

4. What we charge

For transparency, our store launch packages are one-time projects starting at $500 (Starter), $950 (Growth) and $1,500 (Premium). They are starting points, and the final fixed quote depends on products, pages and features. Monthly growth plans for marketing start at $2,000, and ad spend is paid directly to the ad platforms. Prices are in US dollars. See Shopify store design for what is included.

5. How to compare quotes

  1. Ask for the scope in writing: pages, products, features and revisions.
  2. Ask what is not included, such as photography, copywriting, apps and migration.
  3. Ask about mobile design and speed, since most shoppers use phones.
  4. Check what happens after launch: training, support and fixes.
  5. Look at real examples of the provider’s work, and ask to see a store they built, not just a template.
  6. Be wary of very low quotes that skip the basics, and of anyone promising specific sales results.

6. Ongoing costs to plan for

A launch is the start. Budget for the Shopify plan, apps, ads if you use them, content and occasional improvements. A store that is measured and improved earns more than one that is built once and left alone, so see our store audit checklist for what to check after launch.

Common questions

How much does a Shopify store cost to build?

It depends on the size, customization and who builds it. Compare the Shopify plan and apps (monthly) separately from the design and setup (one-time), and ask every provider for a written scope.

Can I build a Shopify store myself?

Yes, with a theme and some time. Many owners do the basics themselves and hire help for design polish, speed, SEO or advertising.

Why do quotes vary so much?

Because the scope differs: number of products, custom features, content, migration and support after launch. Always compare like with like.

Is ad spend included in your packages?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google. Our packages cover design and management only.
