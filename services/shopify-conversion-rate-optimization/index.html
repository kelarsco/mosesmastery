Shopify conversion rate optimization (CRO) & store audits

By Moses Daniel, founder of Moses Mastery

If your store already gets traffic but few orders, the fastest win is usually not more ads. It is fixing the steps where shoppers leave. I audit your funnel, find the biggest leaks and fix them in order of impact.

What a CRO engagement covers

  • Funnel analysis: sessions, product views, add to cart, checkout and purchase, to see where people drop off.
  • Store audit: homepage, navigation, collection and product pages, cart, checkout, speed and trust signals.
  • Prioritized fixes: a ranked list, so the highest-impact changes come first.
  • Implementation: I make the changes, not just a report.
  • Measurement: each change is checked against your numbers, so you know what worked.

Typical problems I find

Slow mobile pages, product pages that bury the add-to-cart button, unclear shipping and returns, weak product photography, reviews placed far from the price, and checkouts with unnecessary friction. The 12-fix Shopify CRO checklist and the store audit checklist show the approach in detail.

What I need from you

Your store link and, ideally, read-only access to Shopify analytics. If you have no tracking set up yet, setting it up is the first step, because it is impossible to improve what is not measured.

Pricing

Frequently asked questions

What is conversion rate optimization for Shopify?

Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is improving your store so a bigger share of visitors become customers, by fixing product pages, navigation, cart, checkout, speed and trust, using your own store data.

How long does CRO take to show results?

Some fixes, like speed and clearer product pages, can be measured within weeks. Reliable conclusions need enough traffic, so smaller stores need longer test periods. No honest agency can promise a specific lift.

Do I need a lot of traffic for CRO to work?

More traffic makes testing faster, but many fixes are obvious usability problems that help at any size. Low-traffic stores focus on clear fixes rather than A/B tests.

Can you audit my Shopify store first?

Yes. Send your store link through the contact page and describe what you want improved. The audit shows where I would start.
