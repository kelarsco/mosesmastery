Shopify store design & development

By Moses Daniel, founder of Moses Mastery

A Shopify store that looks good but does not guide shoppers to the buy button is an expensive brochure. I design and build Shopify stores around one question: what makes this visitor confident enough to buy?

What I design and build

  • New Shopify stores: structure, theme setup, collections, product pages, payments, shipping and policy pages.
  • Theme customization: changing an existing theme so it fits your brand and sells better, without a rebuild.
  • Redesigns: a new look and a better shopping flow for a store that already trades.
  • Product page and cart design: the pages where most buying decisions happen.
  • Store setup basics: payment gateway setup, SSL certificate setup, multi-language and multi-currency setup, and privacy, terms and policy pages.

How a project runs

  1. Discovery. I review your products, audience, competitors and any current store data.
  2. Plan. We agree the page structure, features and what is in scope before design starts.
  3. Design and build. I build in Shopify, with mobile first, because most store traffic is on phones.
  4. Test and launch. Checkout, payments, speed and tracking are checked before going live.
  5. Handover. You get a walkthrough so you can manage products and content yourself.

Who this is for

Founders launching a first Shopify store, brands moving to Shopify from another platform, and store owners whose design no longer matches the quality of their products. If your store gets visitors but few orders, start with a conversion audit instead, since a full redesign is not always the answer. My guide Shopify redesign or optimization? helps you decide.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Shopify store design cost?

Moses Mastery Store Launch packages start from $500 for Starter, $950 for Growth and $1,500 for Premium. The final price depends on the number of products, pages and custom features, and you receive a fixed quote before work starts.

Do you build on a custom theme or customize an existing one?

Both. Many stores do well with a customized premium theme, which is faster and cheaper. Custom features are added where the store really needs them.

Will my store work well on mobile?

Yes. Stores are designed mobile first and checked on real phone sizes, because most Shopify traffic arrives on mobile devices.

Can you redesign my existing Shopify store?

Yes. Redesigns keep your products, apps and data, and improve the layout, product pages and shopping flow.
