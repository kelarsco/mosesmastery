Shopify SEO & Google Merchant Center

By Moses Daniel, founder of Moses Mastery

Search traffic is the cheapest customer source once it works, but it takes time and a clean foundation. I set up your Shopify store so Google can crawl it, understand it and show your products.

What Shopify SEO includes

  • Technical SEO: indexing, canonical URLs, sitemap, duplicate collection and product URLs, redirects and page speed.
  • Keyword research: the phrases real shoppers use for your products and categories.
  • Collection and product page optimization: titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and image alt text.
  • Structured data: product, review and breadcrumb markup, so search engines understand your pages.
  • Google Merchant Center: feed setup and fixes, so products can appear in Google Shopping results.
  • Content: guides and buying advice that answer questions shoppers search for.

Honest expectations

SEO is slow. New or changed pages usually take months to settle, and results depend on competition and links. Anyone who promises a number-one ranking is guessing. What I can do is remove technical barriers, match your pages to real search demand and track progress with Search Console data. My Shopify SEO guide explains the full process.

How it works

  1. Technical and content audit of your store.
  2. A prioritized plan: fixes first, content second.
  3. Implementation of on-page and technical changes.
  4. Merchant Center setup or repair.
  5. Monthly review of Search Console data.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shopify good for SEO?

Yes, with some setup. Shopify handles basics like SSL and sitemaps, but duplicate URLs, thin collection pages, slow apps and poor titles still need attention.

How long does Shopify SEO take?

Expect several months before meaningful organic growth. Technical fixes can help sooner, but rankings depend on competition and content quality.

What is Google Merchant Center and do I need it?

Google Merchant Center is where you upload your product feed so products can appear in Google Shopping and free listings. Most product-based stores benefit from setting it up.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Nobody can guarantee rankings. I focus on the work that improves your chances and report progress with real data.
