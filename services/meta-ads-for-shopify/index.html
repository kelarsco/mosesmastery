Meta & Google ads for Shopify stores

By Moses Daniel, founder of Moses Mastery

Ads work when the store, the tracking and the creative are ready for them. I set up and manage Meta and Google campaigns for Shopify stores so your budget goes to the audiences and products that actually sell.

What is included

  • Tracking first: Meta pixel and conversions API, Google tags and purchase tracking, checked before spending.
  • Campaign structure: prospecting, retargeting and product catalog campaigns.
  • Creative direction: briefs and testing plans for images and video, since creative is the biggest lever on Meta.
  • Retargeting funnels: reaching visitors who viewed products or added to cart.
  • Reporting: clear monthly numbers: spend, revenue, cost per purchase and what to do next.

Before you spend on ads

Ads send traffic to your store, so a weak product page or slow site wastes money. I usually check conversion basics first. Read the Meta ads setup, creative and scaling guide for how I approach it.

Pricing and ad spend

Growth Marketing plans start from $2,000 per month: Launch from $2,000, Accelerate from $6,000 and Elite from $10,500. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and Google and is not included in these prices. Plans are quoted to your store and goals through the contact page.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on Meta ads for a Shopify store?

It depends on your margins and goals. Many stores start with a modest test budget, learn what creative and audiences work, then scale. A quote after reviewing your store gives a realistic range.

Is ad spend included in your price?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and Google. Management fees are separate.

Do you manage both Meta and Google ads?

Yes. Meta covers Facebook and Instagram, and Google covers Search and Shopping, including Merchant Center setup.

Can you promise a return on ad spend?

No. Results depend on the product, the offer, the store and competition. I set clear tracking and report real numbers so decisions are based on data.
