Google Merchant Center setup, optimization & suspension help for Shopify stores

By Moses Daniel, founder of Moses Mastery

Google Merchant Center decides whether your products can appear in free listings and Shopping ads. When it is set up badly, products are disapproved, ads stop or the whole account is suspended. I set it up properly, clean up the feed and fix the store issues behind policy problems.

What I set up and fix

  • Account setup and verification: business information, website claim and the connection to your Shopify store.
  • Product feed quality: product titles, descriptions, images, product identifiers (such as GTIN or brand where they apply), price and availability that match your product pages.
  • Shipping, tax and return settings: configured in Merchant Center to match what your store really offers.
  • Disapproved products: reading the issue Google reports and fixing it at the source, in the feed or on the page.
  • Shopping readiness: checking the store and feed before you start Google Shopping or Performance Max campaigns.

Account suspensions and policy problems

Merchant Center issues usually come from information that is missing, unclear or does not match: contact details, return and shipping policies, a price in the feed that differs from the page, or a checkout that does not look trustworthy. I review the reason Google shows in your account, fix the store and feed problems behind it, and help you prepare a clear request for review. Google makes the final decision, so I cannot promise reinstatement or how long a review takes.

How it works

  1. Review. I check your Merchant Center account, feed status and the store pages Google reads.
  2. Fix list. You get a plain list of what is wrong and what will change.
  3. Implement. I make the fixes in Shopify and in Merchant Center.
  4. Monitor. I watch the account for new warnings after the changes.

Pricing

Merchant Center work is quoted to your store, because an account with a few products is very different from a large catalog. Ongoing Google Shopping and ad management is part of the Growth Marketing plans, which start from $2,000 per month (ad spend is paid directly to Google and is not included).

Frequently asked questions

What is Google Merchant Center?

Google Merchant Center is where you upload your product data so your products can appear in Google free listings and Shopping ads. For Shopify stores it is usually connected through the Google and YouTube channel.

Why was my Merchant Center account suspended?

Common causes include missing or unclear business and contact information, return or shipping policy gaps, product data that does not match the landing page, and checkout or trust problems. The exact reason is shown in your account, and fixing it correctly is what I help with.

Can you guarantee my account will be reinstated?

No. Google decides every review. I fix the problems Google reports and help you prepare the request, but I cannot promise an outcome or a timeline.

Do I need Merchant Center to run Google Shopping ads?

Yes. Shopping and many Performance Max campaigns use the product data in Merchant Center, so a clean feed comes before any ad spend.
