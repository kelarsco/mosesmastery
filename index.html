FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Moses Mastery do?

Moses Mastery designs, builds and grows Shopify stores. Services include store design and development, theme customization and redesigns, conversion rate optimization, Meta and Google Ads, eCommerce SEO and Google Merchant Center, brand design, and AI automation.

Do you only work with Shopify?

Shopify is the focus. Design, development and optimization are built around Shopify stores, and the ads, SEO and AI services are designed to support them.

What AI services do you offer?

Three: AI automation and workflows for repetitive store operations, AI content and creative for product copy, SEO content and ad creative, and AI chatbots and assistants for customer questions and product recommendations.

Which niches have you worked in?

Apparel and streetwear, jewelry, supplements and wellness, skincare, food and drink, and pet. You can browse the brands gallery on this page.

Can you audit my existing store?

Yes. Store audits and performance optimization are part of the service. Send your store link through the contact page and describe what you want improved.

How does pricing work?

The Store Launch and Growth Marketing packages on this page show starting points. Send your project details through the contact page and you will receive a quote based on the scope.

How do I get started?

Book a strategy call, or use the contact page to request a quote. Include your store link, your goals and any deadlines so the first reply can be specific.
