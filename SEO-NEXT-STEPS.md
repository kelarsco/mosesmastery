# SEO: what is done and what only you can do

Done in the site: unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs on mosesmastery.com, sitemap, robots (AI crawlers allowed), structured data (organization, services, FAQ, articles, breadcrumbs, social profiles), image alt text, fast-loading media, mobile layout, llms.txt, and a blog.

No one can guarantee position #1 on Google or in ChatGPT. Rankings depend on competition, links and time (usually 3-6 months). These are the steps that move the needle, in order:

1. Google Search Console: add `https://mosesmastery.com`, verify (send me the meta tag and I will add it), submit `sitemap.xml`, and request indexing for the home page and each blog article.
2. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console. Bing powers much of ChatGPT search.
3. Redirect the old mosesecom.com to mosesmastery.com with a 301 redirect in your domain settings.
4. Consistent profiles: same name, site link and description on Instagram, X, Pinterest, Telegram, LinkedIn, Shopify Experts marketplace, Clutch, Upwork/Fiverr. These are also backlinks.
5. Real reviews: ask happy clients for written testimonials with permission; then restore the testimonials section.
6. Publish 2 articles a month on questions store owners actually search; link each to Contact.
7. Earn links: guest posts, Shopify community answers, podcasts, partner agencies.
