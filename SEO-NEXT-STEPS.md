# SEO: what is done and what only you can do

Done in the site: unique titles/descriptions (titles kept near 60 characters), canonical URLs on mosesmastery.com, sitemap, robots (AI crawlers allowed), llms.txt, structured data (organization, person, services, FAQ, articles, breadcrumbs), 5 dedicated service pages + a services hub (one page per search intent), 6 blog articles, internal links between services, articles and contact, image alt text, mobile layout, fast media loading.

No one can guarantee position #1 on Google, ChatGPT or any platform. Rankings depend on competition, links, content and time (usually 3-6 months). These steps move the needle, in order:

1. Google Search Console: add `https://mosesmastery.com`, verify (send me the meta tag and I will add it), submit `sitemap.xml`, then use "Request indexing" on the home page, /services/ and each service and blog page.
2. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console. Bing powers much of ChatGPT search and Copilot.
3. Redirect the old mosesecom.com to mosesmastery.com with a 301 redirect in your domain settings, and make `www.` redirect to the non-www address.
4. Google Business Profile (if you serve clients by location or want a brand panel) and consistent profiles: same name, site link and description on Instagram, X, Pinterest, Telegram, LinkedIn, Clutch, Shopify Experts, Upwork/Fiverr. These are also backlinks.
5. Real reviews: ask happy clients for written testimonials with permission; then I restore the testimonials section and add review markup.
6. Publish 2 articles a month on questions store owners search; link each to a service page and Contact.
7. Earn links: guest posts, Shopify community answers, podcasts, partner agencies, client store credits ("designed by Moses Mastery") with permission.
8. Check Search Console monthly: which queries show impressions but low clicks, and improve those titles and pages.
